## Thesis

Arista is a fast-growing, highly profitable network-as-a-service platform (EOS/NetDL) with leadership in high-speed Ethernet switching for AI, cloud, campus, and WAN, but the bull case must now be read alongside two structural tensions the red team surfaced: revenue is concentrated in two large end customers rather than being genuinely diversified across categories, and the company's 'open, vendor-lock-in-free' differentiation depends on a single merchant silicon supplier (Broadcom) that could itself become a competitive threat via white-box alternatives. The financial profile remains excellent, but a premium valuation is being paid for growth that management itself says is difficult to forecast. <sub>[S1] [S2] [S28] [S24] [S26]</sub>

**PM's rationale:** The financial profile (28.6% revenue growth, 42.8% operating margin, 47.2% FCF margin, 28.4% ROE, minimal dilution) is genuinely strong, but several high-severity red team objections — customer concentration (two customers at up to 26% and 16% of revenue), a differentiation narrative in tension with sole-source Broadcom dependency, and a premium valuation (71.38x P/E, 27.83x EV/revenue) resting on demand management itself calls unpredictable — are not fully addressed by public filings alone and require targeted diligence before forming a durable view. <sub>[F46] [F42] [F49] [F45] [S28] [S24] [F52] [F55] [S31]</sub>

## Bull case

- Revenue grew from $4.4B in FY2022 to $9.0B in FY2025, a 28.6% YoY increase and 27.1% three-year CAGR, reflecting strong demand for AI/cloud/data center networking. <sub>[F1] [F4] [F46] [F47]</sub>
- Profitability scaled with growth: gross margin 64.1%, operating margin 42.8%, net margin 39.0% in FY2025, with operating income rising from $1.5B in FY2022 to $3.9B in FY2025. <sub>[F41] [F42] [F43] [F9] [F12]</sub>
- Cash conversion is strong: $4.4B operating cash flow and $4.3B free cash flow (47.2% FCF margin) in FY2025, up sharply from $492.8M operating cash flow in FY2022, on a still-modest capex base. <sub>[F24] [F48] [F49] [F21]</sub>
- Capital efficiency is high with minimal dilution: ROE of 28.4% in FY2025, stockholders' equity growing from $4.9B (FY2022) to $12.4B (FY2025), and diluted shares essentially flat (1.27B to 1.28B), down 0.4% YoY. <sub>[F45] [F33] [F36] [F37] [F40] [F50]</sub>

## Bear case (after red-team review)

- Revenue is highly concentrated: one end customer represented 26%, 20%, and 18% of total revenue and another 16%, 15%, and 21% across 2025, 2024, and 2023 — a swing in either customer's order timing could materially move total revenue despite the thesis's framing of diversified customer categories. <sub>[S28] [S39]</sub>
- The core 'open technology, no vendor lock-in' differentiation is undercut by explicit dependence on a single merchant silicon vendor, Broadcom, which management warns 'may become competitive with us by selling merchant silicon for white boxes with open-source network operating systems.' <sub>[S24] [S26]</sub>
- Gross margin held flat at 64.1% in both 2025 and 2024, but management attributes ongoing pressure to large-customer pricing discounts that 'reduce gross margins in the periods when such sales occur' and to 'supply chain inflation and material scarcity, such as the recent tightening of supply conditions in the memory market' — flat margins may mask offsetting headwinds rather than reflect durable pricing power. <sub>[S40] [S44] [F41]</sub>
- Management explicitly states AI demand's 'long-term trajectory is unknown' and that 'demand estimates for our new products are difficult to forecast and can create volatility,' while customers simultaneously pursue AI investment and non-AI capex cost-reduction — a direct tension with relying on the 27.1% three-year CAGR as a forward indicator. <sub>[S31] [F47]</sub>
- Capital expenditures more than tripled year-over-year, from $32.0M in FY2024 to $119.5M in FY2025, a shift not explained by the thesis and a potential early signal of rising capital intensity. <sub>[F27] [F28]</sub>
- New product introductions and customer trial/acceptance arrangements are flagged as creating 'increased volatility and magnitude of... product deferred revenue balances' and risk of inventory write-downs if trial requirements aren't met — an earnings-quality risk not reflected in the cash-conversion narrative. <sub>[S41]</sub>
- At a $250.6B market cap, the stock trades at 71.38x trailing net income and 27.83x EV/revenue, embedding high growth expectations even as management characterizes forward AI-driven demand as fundamentally unpredictable. <sub>[F51] [F52] [F55] [S31]</sub>
