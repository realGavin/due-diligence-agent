## Thesis

Deere is a quality cyclical mid-downturn in its core equipment business, with revenue down from a FY2023 peak of $61.3B to $45.7B in FY2025 (-11.7% YoY, -4.6% 3-yr CAGR) and net income falling to $5.0B from $10.2B in FY2023. The balance sheet has strengthened (equity to $25.9B, cash to $8.3B) and buybacks have cut diluted shares to 271.7M from 306.3M in FY2022, but red-team review shows this buyback-driven per-share improvement partly masks underlying earnings deterioration rather than reflecting improving returns on new capital. Valuation is demanding on both an earnings basis (P/E 37.08x) and a cash-flow basis (FCF yield 3.3%, P/FCF 30.56x), so there is no obvious 'cheap cash flow' offset to the depressed-earnings P/E, and the business remains structurally exposed to macro-driven cyclicality via its PPA segment (45% of equipment operations net sales) tied to farm cash receipts, commodity prices, trade policy, interest rates, and weather. This is a hold/watch, not a value entry point, until either earnings inflect or valuation compresses meaningfully. <sub>[F2] [F4] [F40] [F41] [F10] [F12] [F32] [F28] [F36] [F33] [F46] [F48] [F47] [S9]</sub>

## Bull case

- Despite the downturn, Deere converts earnings into cash well above reported net income: FY2025 operating cash flow of $7.5B and free cash flow of $6.1B (13.4% FCF margin) versus net income of $5.0B, with capex of only $1.4B indicating modest capital intensity. <sub>[F20] [F42] [F43] [F24] [F12]</sub>
- The balance sheet has strengthened through the cycle: stockholders' equity rose to $25.9B in FY2025 from $20.3B in FY2022, and cash grew to $8.3B from $7.3B in FY2024. <sub>[F32] [F29] [F28] [F27]</sub>
- Capital returns continue via buybacks, with diluted shares falling to 271.7M in FY2025 from 306.3M in FY2022 (-1.9% YoY most recently), supporting per-share metrics. <sub>[F36] [F33] [F44]</sub>
- R&D spend has held at $2.3B (5.1% of revenue) in FY2025, matching FY2024 dollar levels, indicating management is sustaining investment in the Smart Industrial Operating Model's technology stack (automation, connectivity, data platforms) rather than cutting through the downturn. <sub>[F16] [F15] [F38] [S4] [S5]</sub>
- Deere's moat extends beyond equipment sales into an integrated aftermarket, parts, and lifecycle services model plus a dealer channel intended to capture value across the full ownership life of equipment, diversifying revenue beyond new-unit cyclicality. <sub>[S6] [S7]</sub>

## Bear case (after red-team review)

- Valuation is rich on cash-flow terms too, not just on depressed-earnings P/E: FCF yield is only 3.3% and P/FCF is 30.56x, so there is no 'cheap on cash flow' offset to the 37.08x P/E, undermining the idea that the stock is a bargain waiting for an earnings inflection. <sub>[F48] [F47] [F46]</sub>
- PPA alone represents 45% of equipment operations net sales and is explicitly exposed to farm cash receipts, commodity prices, government trade policy, interest rates, and weather — uncontrollable macro factors that could extend the downturn well beyond a simple two-year trough. <sub>[S9] [S17]</sub>
- The 19.4% ROE and per-share metric improvements are partly mechanical: diluted shares fell from 306.3M (FY2022) to 271.7M (FY2025) even as net income fell from $10.2B (FY2023) to $5.0B (FY2025), meaning buybacks flatter ROE/EPS without necessarily reflecting better returns on new capital. <sub>[F36] [F33] [F12] [F10] [F39]</sub>
- Revenue and profit have now declined for multiple consecutive periods — revenue from $61.3B (FY2023) to $51.7B (FY2024) to $45.7B (FY2025), and operating income from $13.0B (FY2023) to $9.0B (FY2024) — confirming the deterioration is operational, not just a single bad year. <sub>[F2] [F3] [F4] [F7] [F8]</sub>
- Aggregate balance-sheet strength (equity to $25.9B, cash to $8.3B) does not capture segment-specific working-capital risk: PPA and SAT must finance inventory and dealer sales well ahead of seasonal retail demand, creating cash-timing exposure tied to planting/harvest cycles that could tighten further in a prolonged downturn. <sub>[F32] [F28] [S22] [S23]</sub>
